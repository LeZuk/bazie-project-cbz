f_lastnames.csv - 1000 most popular polish female surnames with probability
f_names.csv - 200 most popular polish female names with probability
m_lastnames.csv - 1000 most popular polish male surnames with probability
m_names.csv - 200 most popular polish male names with probability

probabilities were normalized to add up to 1;
Which mean we assume that there aren't any other names/surnames than those present in the files;
